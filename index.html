Coupon Types

Welcome Coupons vs Promotional Coupons

Two coupon types designed for different goals — acquisition and retention.

Acquisition

Welcome Coupons

Convert first visits into verified contacts. Targets new and first-time customers with a one-time welcome incentive. Best for new shop launches and first-visit conversion.

Re-Marketing

Promotional Coupons

Drive repeat visits and fill slow periods. Targets existing members with time-bound offers. Perfect for weekday lunch, off-peak boosts, seasonal promos, and member-only deals.
