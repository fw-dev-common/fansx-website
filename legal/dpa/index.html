Data Processing Agreement

Last updated 17 August 2026

1. Parties and structure

Processor: FansX Limited ("FansX") Data User / Customer: the merchant (the "Customer")

This DPA is intended to be incorporated by reference into the FansX Customer Terms of Service, and to be supplemented for enterprise customers by an executed Order Form.

2. Definitions

"Member Data" means personal data of the Customer's programme members processed through the Services, including: mobile number; name; email address; messaging-platform display name and account identifier; message content exchanged with the Customer's account; receipt images and purchase details extracted from them; loyalty points and transaction history; coupon issuance and redemption records; consent records; IP address; and social-follow status.

"Personal Data Breach" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Member Data. It does not include unsuccessful attempts or activities that do not result in unauthorised access to or acquisition of Member Data, including unsuccessful log-in attempts, pings and port scans.

"PDPO" means the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486).

"this DPA" means this Data Processing Agreement, including its Schedules.

Capitalised terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the FansX Customer Terms of Service.

3. Roles

3.1 The Customer is the data user in respect of Member Data. The Customer determines the purposes for which and the manner in which Member Data is collected, held, processed and used.

3.2 Save as stated in clause 3.4, FansX is a data processor, processing Member Data on behalf of the Customer and on the Customer's documented instructions, and makes no independent use of Member Data save as expressly permitted under this DPA.

3.3 The Customer acknowledges that, under section 65(2) of the PDPO, acts done by FansX as the Customer's agent are treated as done by the Customer as well as by FansX.

3.4 In respect of the creation of anonymised or aggregated data under clause 6 — being processing of Member Data for FansX's own purposes — FansX acts as a data user in its own right and shall comply with the PDPO in that capacity. The resulting anonymised or aggregated data is not personal data.

4. Customer obligations

4.1 The Customer warrants that it has given the notices and obtained the consents required under the PDPO — including a Personal Information Collection Statement under DPP1(3) and, where applicable, direct-marketing consent under section 35C — before Member Data is processed, and that those notices cover the processing described in this DPA, including the creation and use of anonymised and aggregated data under clause 6.

4.2 The Customer's instructions shall not require FansX to act in breach of the PDPO.

4.3 Compliance with Part 6A of the PDPO (direct marketing) is the responsibility of the Customer as data user. FansX acts solely on the Customer's instructions in this respect.

4.4 The Customer remains responsible for responding to data access requests under section 18 and data correction requests under section 22 of the PDPO. FansX shall provide reasonable assistance, using the functionality available in the Services.

4.5 Assistance beyond FansX's obligations. Where the Customer requests assistance, information or action which is not an obligation of FansX under this DPA — including assistance going beyond the functionality available in the Services — FansX may charge for the resources, time and materials reasonably required. FansX shall notify the Customer of the charge before incurring it and, so far as practicable, provide an estimate. Where the Customer does not agree to the charge, FansX is not required to perform the task and is not in breach of this DPA by reason of not performing it.

4.6 Standing instruction — direct Member requests. The Customer authorises and instructs FansX, as a standing instruction under this DPA, to act without further instruction on a request received directly from a Member through the Services or a Third-Party Platform: to unsubscribe the Member from, or suppress, direct marketing messages sent through the Services, and, where the Member so requests, to delete that Member's Member Data.

5. FansX obligations

5.1 FansX shall comply with the obligations set out in Schedule 1 (Data Protection Clauses), which adopt the Privacy Commissioner's Recommended Model Contractual Clauses (data user to data processor, May 2022).

5.2 FansX shall ensure that personnel authorised to process Member Data are bound by obligations of confidentiality.

5.3 FansX shall implement the security measures described in Schedule 3.

5.4 For the purposes of paragraphs 4 and 5 of Schedule 1, Member Data is required for the processing instructed, and remains so, until the Customer instructs its deletion. FansX is not required to determine independently whether Member Data remains necessary for the Customer's purposes, that determination being the Customer's as data user.

5.5 For the purposes of paragraphs 6 and 7 of Schedule 1, Member Data is treated as accurate as supplied by the Member or by the Customer. FansX is not required to verify Member Data independently or to monitor it for accuracy.

6. Aggregated and anonymised data

6.1 The Customer instructs and permits FansX to create anonymised or aggregated data from Member Data, being data which does not identify, and cannot reasonably be used to identify, the Customer, any member or any other individual.

6.2 FansX may use such data for the purposes of analytics, benchmarking, fraud prevention, and the development and improvement of FansX's products and services, including analysis across FansX's customers.

6.3 FansX shall not attempt to re-identify such data.

6.4 Such data does not constitute Member Data or personal data, and FansX may continue to use and retain it following termination or expiry of this DPA.

6.5 FansX shall not disclose data identifying the Customer or any individual to any third party, save as expressly permitted under this DPA.

6.6 To the extent any anonymised or aggregated data described in this clause, or any output derived from it, is made available to the Customer, the Customer shall not sell, resell or licence it to any third party.

7. Automated processing and model development

7.1 The Customer acknowledges that receipt validation and related features are performed by automated means, including machine-learning models, and that FansX engages AI and machine-learning service providers to deliver those features.

7.2 FansX may change such providers from time to time, subject to clause 8.

7.3 Model development. The rights granted by clause 6.2 include the development, training, evaluation and improvement of software, models and algorithms, whether by FansX or by a service provider engaged by FansX for that purpose. FansX may continue to use any model so developed following termination or expiry of this DPA.

7.4 The Customer shall not knowingly permit members to submit material containing full payment-card numbers or identity-card numbers.

7.5 Sensitive data. The Services are not designed for sensitive personal data — including data concerning health, biometrics, minors or financial accounts — beyond the categories described in clause 2. The Customer shall not use the Services to collect, hold or process such data. If such data is nonetheless submitted, it is processed as Member Data on the terms of this DPA, and FansX assumes no obligation or liability in respect of it additional to those terms.

8. Sub-processors

8.1 The Customer grants FansX general authorisation to engage sub-processors falling within the categories identified in the Data Transfer Schedule.

8.2 FansX shall impose on each sub-processor data-protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains responsible for its own compliance with this Agreement. FansX is liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-processors in the performance of this DPA to the same extent as if they were its own.

8.3 FansX shall, on the Customer's reasonable written request, provide details of the sub-processors then engaged. FansX is not required to publish or maintain a list of sub-processors, to give notice of a change, or to obtain the Customer's consent to a change within a category identified in the Data Transfer Schedule.

8.4 Member Data at rest is hosted in a Permitted Jurisdiction, currently Singapore. FansX may change the hosting location or hosting provider, provided the location remains within the Permitted Jurisdictions.

8.5 A Third-Party Platform through which the Customer communicates with Members — including the operator of a messaging or social platform — acts under the Customer's own relationship with that platform and is not a sub-processor of FansX.

9. Personal data breach

9.1 FansX shall notify the Customer without undue delay after FansX confirms that a Personal Data Breach affecting Member Data has occurred.

9.2 Such notice shall include the information reasonably available to FansX at the time.

9.3 FansX's response to, or notice of, a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgment of fault or liability.

10. Retention

10.1 FansX retains Member Data for the duration of the Customer's subscription and thereafter in accordance with the Retention Period stated in the Data Transfer Schedule and clause 11.

10.2 The Customer, as data user, is responsible for determining the retention periods appropriate to its purposes.

11. Termination, return and deletion

11.1 The Customer may export Member Data using the export functionality available in the Services at any time during the term.

11.2 FansX shall not disable the export functionality for 30 days following termination or expiry, after which FansX may delete, return or anonymise Member Data, at FansX's election.

11.3 FansX may retain Member Data where required by law, and copies held in routine backups may persist until those backups expire in the ordinary course.

11.4 FansX is not required to provide a certificate or other confirmation of destruction, save where expressly agreed in an Order Form.

11.5 The scope of the export functionality is set out in Schedule 4 (Export Deliverable).

12. Liability

12.1 Liability under this DPA is governed by clause 12 of the FansX Customer Terms of Service (the General Terms), which applies once across the General Terms, all Modules and this DPA.

12.1A Direct claims. The Customer shall indemnify FansX for the amount by which FansX's aggregate liability for claims brought directly by Members or by the Customer's affiliates, arising from or connected with the Customer's programme, exceeds the amount for which FansX would have been liable to the Customer under clause 12.1 had the same loss been claimed by the Customer.

12.2 For the avoidance of doubt, liability arising from a Personal Data Breach or from breach of the data-protection obligations in this DPA is subject to that cap.

12.3 Nothing in this DPA excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for any other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

FansX maintains technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including measures in each of the following categories. FansX may vary the specific measures within a category provided the overall level of protection is not materially reduced.

1. Encryption in transit. Member Data transmitted between the Customer, Members and the Services, and between the Services and sub-processors, is protected in transit using transport-layer security (TLS). The captive-portal architecture requires certain network-authentication and captive-portal detection requests to be served over HTTP; those requests are limited to network identifiers (a device's IP and MAC address) and do not carry the Customer's programme Member Data.

2. Encryption and protection at rest. Member Data at rest is held on managed database and object-storage infrastructure with encryption at rest enabled at the storage layer.

3. Access control and authentication. Access to Member Data is restricted to authorised personnel and to the Customer's own authorised users, on a least-privilege basis; administrative functions are gated, and an administrative view-only mode is available.

4. Segregation. Each Customer's Member Data is logically segregated by organisation identifier across the Services, and production environments are segregated from non-production environments.

5. Logging and monitoring. The Services generate operational logs used for diagnostics, security monitoring and the investigation of suspected incidents; an append-only consent record is maintained.

6. Sub-processor assurance. Sub-processors are engaged under the flow-down obligations in clause 8 and Schedule 1 paragraph 9, and are limited to the categories and Permitted Jurisdictions identified in the Data Transfer Schedule.

7. Vulnerability and change management. FansX maintains a patch and vulnerability-management practice: patches and updates are applied, and identified vulnerabilities addressed, on a commercially reasonable, ordinary-course basis. This is a statement of practice and not a warranty that the Services will be free of vulnerabilities.

8. Personnel. Personnel authorised to process Member Data are bound by confidentiality (clause 5.2).

1. Export Deliverable. On request during the term, and using the export functionality in the Services, the Customer may obtain a member-record export in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV or equivalent), comprising for each Member: profile and contact fields (name, mobile number, email address where held), messaging-platform account identifier(s), membership status and enrolment date, and current loyalty-point balance.

2. Not included in the standard export. The following elements of Member Data are not covered by the export functionality currently available, and are made available, if at all, only by separate arrangement: full loyalty-points and transaction history (as distinct from the current balance); coupon issuance and redemption history; receipt images and the purchase details extracted from them; message content; and consent records.

3. Format and delivery. The export is provided in the format and by the means the Services make available. FansX does not undertake to provide Member Data in any particular schema, or via any interface other than the export functionality in the Services, except under an Order Form.

4. Post-termination. Clause 11 governs availability of the export functionality following termination or expiry.
