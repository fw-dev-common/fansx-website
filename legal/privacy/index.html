Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

1. Who we are

1.1 This Privacy Policy and Personal Information Collection Statement is issued by FansX Limited ("FansX", "we") pursuant to the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) ("PDPO"). It explains how we collect and use personal data in respect of which we are the data user.

1.2 Questions, and the requests described in clause 9, should be directed to support@fansx.io or by post to 6/F, Core C, Cyberport 3, 100 Cyberport Road, Pok Fu Lam, Hong Kong.

2. What this Policy does not cover — programme members

2.1 Where you are a member of a merchant's loyalty or membership programme operated through the FansX platform, the merchant — not FansX — is the data user in respect of your personal data. Your data is governed by that merchant's own terms and collection statement, which were presented to you when you joined its programme. FansX processes that data only on the merchant's instructions, as its data processor, and does not use it for FansX's own marketing or sell it to anyone. FansX may create anonymised statistics that do not identify you, as described in the merchant's programme terms.

2.2 Requests to access or correct programme-member data should be made to the merchant, as set out in the programme's terms. If you contact us instead, we will refer your request to the merchant.

3. Whose data we hold, and what we collect

3.1 Merchant account data. Where you register for or administer a FansX account on behalf of a business: your name, business email address and phone number, role, login and security records, and the content of your communications with us.

3.2 Billing data. Billing contact details, invoicing records and payment records. Card payments are processed by our payment service provider; we do not store full card numbers.

3.3 Usage data. Technical records of the use of our services and websites — logs, diagnostics, device and browser information, IP address, and feature usage — as described in our terms of service.

3.4 Sales and enquiry data. Where you contact us or request a demonstration: your name, business, contact details and the content of your enquiry.

3.5 Supply of the data in clauses 3.1 and 3.2 is necessary: without it we cannot open or administer an account or bill for the services, and you or your business will not be able to use them. Supply of any other data is voluntary.

4. What we use it for

4.1 We use the data described in clause 3 to: provide, secure and administer the services; verify identity and manage accounts; invoice and collect payment; provide support and communicate about the services, including service and security notices; monitor use in accordance with our Fair Use Policy; prevent fraud and abuse; improve and develop our products; comply with legal obligations; and establish or defend legal claims.

4.2 Service and administrative communications (for example invoices, security notices and notices of changes to terms) are not direct marketing and are sent for as long as the account exists.

5. Direct marketing

5.1 We intend to use your name and contact details, and records of your business's use of our services, to send you direct marketing about the following classes of goods and services: FansX's software platform and related products, features, pricing, events and educational content. We may not so use your data without your consent (which includes an indication of no objection).

5.2 You may at any time, free of charge, require us to stop, by using the unsubscribe facility in any message or by contacting support@fansx.io. We will not provide your personal data to any other person for that person's direct marketing without your written consent.

6. Who we may share it with

6.1 Your data may be transferred, for the purposes in clause 4, to: members of FansX's group of companies; service providers engaged by FansX, including cloud hosting and storage, payment processing, communications and email delivery, error monitoring, and analytics providers; our professional advisers and insurers; a purchaser or prospective purchaser in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets; and courts, regulators and law enforcement where required or permitted by law.

6.2 Your data may be processed in the jurisdictions in which we and the providers in clause 6.1 operate, currently including Hong Kong, Singapore and the United States. We take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected consistently with this Policy wherever it is processed.

7. Retention

7.1 We keep the data described in this Policy for as long as the relevant account or relationship is active and thereafter as needed for the purposes in clause 4, including for as long as required for legal, accounting or record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Security

8.1 We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss and misuse. No internet service can be guaranteed secure, and transmission is at your own risk.

9. Your rights

9.1 Under sections 18 and 22 of the PDPO you may request access to, and correction of, your personal data held by us. Requests should be made to the contact in clause 1.2. We may charge a reasonable fee for complying with a data access request. For programme-member data, clause 2 applies: requests should be made to the merchant.

10. Cookies

10.1 Our websites use cookies and similar technologies for essential site functions and to measure how the sites are used, and may in future also use analytics and advertising technologies, including third-party pixels (such as those of Meta or Google). You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect site functionality.

11. Changes and language

11.1 We may update this Policy by publishing a revised version with a new effective date.

11.2 Where this Policy is published in English and Chinese, the English version prevails.
