Service Level Agreement

Last updated 17 August 2026

1. Application

1.1 This Service Level Agreement applies only where expressly incorporated by an Order Form executed between FansX Limited ("FansX") and the Customer.

1.2 Capitalised terms have the meanings given in the FansX Customer Terms of Service.

1.3 This SLA applies to the availability of the Services. It does not apply to any Third-Party Platform.

2. Availability commitment

2.1 FansX will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services Available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, measured as set out in clause 3.

2.2 "Available" means the Services are capable of accepting and responding to requests. The Services are not treated as unavailable during any period falling within clause 4.

3. Measurement

3.1 Monthly Availability is calculated as:

(Total Minutes in Month − Excluded Minutes − Unavailable Minutes) ÷ (Total Minutes in Month − Excluded Minutes) × 100

where Unavailable Minutes are minutes during which the Services were not Available, and Excluded Minutes are minutes falling within clause 4.

3.2 FansX does not undertake to monitor, calculate or report Availability on an ongoing basis. Availability is determined only in response to a valid claim under clause 5, by reference to FansX's own logs and monitoring records, which are the primary evidence of Availability.

4. Exclusions

4.1 The following do not count as Unavailable Minutes and are Excluded Minutes:

(a) scheduled maintenance notified to the Customer at least 24 hours in advance;

(b) emergency maintenance reasonably required to preserve the security or integrity of the Services;

(c) unavailability, degradation, rate limiting, policy change, account restriction or suspension of, or caused by, any Third-Party Platform, including WhatsApp, Instagram and any messaging, social, payment or artificial-intelligence service provider, except to the extent the restriction or suspension resulted from FansX's breach of the Agreement;

(d) failure of the Customer's own systems, network, devices, credentials or configuration, or acts or omissions of the Customer or its personnel or authorised users;

(e) suspension of the Services in accordance with the General Terms, including for non-payment or to prevent harm;

(f) use of the Services otherwise than in accordance with the General Terms or documentation, or use of features identified as beta, trial or preview;

(g) failures of general internet infrastructure, or of any network or facility not within FansX's direct control; and

(h) force majeure.

5. Claims

5.1 If the Customer believes the Availability commitment was not met in a calendar month, it may submit a claim by email to support@fansx.io.

5.2 A claim must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the month to which it relates, and must specify the dates and times of the unavailability claimed and the Services affected.

5.3 A claim submitted after that period, or without that detail, is not valid and no Service Credit is payable.

5.4 FansX will verify the claim against its records within 30 days and notify the Customer of the outcome.

6. Service Credits

6.1 Where a valid claim establishes that Availability fell below 99.5% in a calendar month, the Customer is entitled to a Service Credit as follows:

Monthly AvailabilityService Credit
Below 99.5% but at or above 99.0%3 days' extension
Below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%7 days' extension
Below 95.0%15 days' extension

6.2 A "Service Credit" is a non-refundable, non-transferable extension, for the number of days stated and at no additional subscription charge, of (a) the Customer's current subscription period under the General Terms, and (b) — notwithstanding clause A5(f) of Schedule A to the General Terms or any credit-expiry period stated in an Order Form — the expiry date of any prepaid credit that is unexpired when the Service Credit is granted. It has no cash or monetary value, is not a credit against any invoice or charge, and does not increase any credit balance or usage-based entitlement or affect any Third-Party Platform charge — usage-based fees and credit consumption continue to accrue and be charged in the ordinary way during the extension.

6.3 Service Credits in any 12-month period shall not exceed 30 days in aggregate. A Service Credit takes effect only if the Customer's subscription is active when it is granted.

6.4 Service Credits are not payable or redeemable in cash, are not refundable, may not be exchanged for any fee reduction, and lapse — without compensation — on termination or expiry of the subscription for any reason.

6.5 Subject to clause 12.1 of the General Terms, Service Credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure by FansX to meet the Availability commitment, and are in place of any claim for damages, refund, fee reduction or other loss arising from unavailability of the Services. Nothing in this clause limits either party's rights under clause 14.2, or the Customer's right of non-renewal under clause 14.4, of the General Terms.

8. Changes

8.1 FansX may amend this Service Level Agreement on 30 days' notice, provided the amendment does not reduce the Availability commitment in clause 2.1 or the Service Credits in clause 6.1 during the term of the Order Form incorporating it.
