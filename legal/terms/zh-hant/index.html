2. 定義

「協議」（"Agreement"） 指本一般條款、適用的模組、資料處理協議、合理使用政策、可接受使用政策、 人工智能服務附錄（如適用）、任何經訂購表格納入的服務水平協議，以及任何訂購表格。

「保密資料」（"Confidential Information"） 指一方向另一方披露、經註明為保密或按合理人的理解屬 保密的資料，包括客戶的業務及會員資料，以及 FansX 的技術及定價資料。

「資料處理協議」（"Data Processing Agreement"） 指 FansX 資料處理協議，經第 1.2 條納入本協議。

「會員」（"Member"） 指參加客戶的忠誠計劃，或以其他方式透過本服務與客戶互動的個人。

「會員資料」（"Member Data"） 具有資料處理協議所賦予的涵義。

「模組」（"Module"） 指本一般條款項下的產品專屬附表。

「訂購表格」（"Order Form"） 指由雙方簽署、載明所訂購的模組、費用及期限的文件。

「服務水平協議」（"Service Level Agreement"） 指經已簽署的訂購表格明文納入的服務水平協議。

「本服務」（"Services"） 指 FansX 平台及所訂購的模組。

「平台費用」（"Platform Fees"） 具有第 12.4 條所賦予的涵義。

「訂閱費用」（"Subscription Fees"） 具有第 12.4(a) 條所賦予的涵義。

「第三方平台」（"Third-Party Platform"） 指本服務藉以或透過其運作的訊息、社交、支付、人工智能或 機器學習平台或服務供應商，包括 WhatsApp、Instagram 及任何 AI 或 OCR 服務供應商。

Translator's notes for counsel

The following renderings are accurate to the English but could carry a shade of legal meaning a HK lawyer should confirm before the Chinese version is published, even as a reference translation.

  1. Clause 12.2 — "indirect or consequential loss" → 「間接或相應而生的損失」. The English

phrase carries the *Hadley v Baxendale* second-limb case-law meaning; the Chinese rendering is the conventional HK equivalent but has no equivalent body of case law fixing its scope. Counsel should confirm the Chinese wording is not read more broadly (or narrowly) than the English term of art.

  1. Clause 14.2 — "an arrangement or composition with its creditors generally" →

「與其債權人整體達成債務安排或債務重整」. "Composition" has a specific insolvency meaning (cf. Bankruptcy Ordinance (Cap. 6) terminology); 債務重整 is a serviceable rendering but is not a BLIS-anchored term and could be read as the broader "debt restructuring". Counsel may prefer the statutory wording used in Cap. 6 / Cap. 32 for the intended trigger events.

  1. Clause 9.8 — "unaided memory" → 「無輔助記憶」. There is no established Chinese term of

art for the US-style residuals concept; a literal rendering was used. Counsel should confirm it conveys "without reference to records" rather than merely "imperfect memory".

  1. Clause 12.5 — "recovery of a cost incurred by FansX in respect of a third party" →

「代表 FansX 收回其就第三方所招致成本的款項」. The English is a characterisation of the amount ("represents the recovery of..."); the Chinese necessarily restructures the phrase. Counsel should confirm the exclusion still catches pass-through amounts however characterised, since this definition drives the 25% liability cap base.

  1. Schedule A2 — "Configuration set, published or left in effect by the Customer" →

「客戶透過本服務設定、發布或維持生效的配置」. "Left in effect" imports deemed approval by *inaction*; 「維持生效」 could be read as implying a positive act of maintaining. If the deemed-approval-by-omission effect is important, counsel may wish to strengthen to e.g. 「或任由其持續生效」.

  1. Schedule A8(a) — "stored value facility" → 「儲值支付工具」. This is the exact statutory

term from the Payment Systems and Stored Value Facilities Ordinance (Cap. 584). Using the statutory term makes the disclaimer track the Cap. 584 licensing regime precisely — which is presumably intended — but counsel should confirm the English "stored value facility" is meant in that statutory sense and not any wider sense.

  1. Clause 6.2 / 6.3 — "collection notice" → 「收集個人資料聲明」. The English uses

"collection notice" (generic) while the glossary term is the rendering of "Personal Information Collection Statement (PICS)". The Chinese collapses the two into the single PDPO-conventional term. This is almost certainly the intent, but counsel should confirm no distinction between "collection notice" and "PICS" is intended across the document set.
