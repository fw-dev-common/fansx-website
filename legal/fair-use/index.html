Fair Use Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026

1. Purpose

1.1 This Policy sets out what constitutes fair use of the FansX Services. It applies to every Customer and forms part of the Agreement. Capitalised terms have the meanings given in the FansX Customer Terms of Service. In the event of inconsistency, the General Terms prevail.

1.2 Its purpose is to keep the Services available and performing consistently for all Customers.

2. Expected use

2.1 The Services are provided for the operation of the Customer's own loyalty and messaging programme, for its own Members, within the limits of its subscription plan.

2.2 The Customer shall not generate load — whether by automation, integration, bulk import or otherwise — that materially degrades the Services for other Customers.

2.3 The qualitative conduct rules — lawful use, content, messaging and anti-spam, Third-Party Platform compliance, security, proprietary rights and credentials — are set out in the FansX Acceptable Use Policy, which also forms part of the Agreement.

3. Fair use and limits

3.1 The Customer's use of the Services must be reasonable and consistent with its subscription plan. The plan states the Member cap for the Customer (for the standard plan, 10,000 Members).

3.2 No free allowance for cost-bearing services. The Services do not include any free allowance of Third-Party Platform messaging, conversation, delivery, or of any other service that carries a per-use or fixed cost. Such charges are payable in accordance with clause 7.4 of the General Terms.

3.3 FansX does not publish fixed numeric caps for outbound messages, receipt submissions, API requests, storage or similar usage. FansX assesses whether the Customer's use is fair, acting reasonably and having regard in particular to the load it places on the Services' infrastructure (including server, database and storage resources), the cost it imposes on FansX, and its effect on other Customers. FansX may take any step in clause 4 where the Customer's use is, in FansX's reasonable assessment, not fair use — including where it is excessive, disproportionate in cost, or degrading to the Services.

3.4 Usage is assessed per Customer account, across all venues and brands operated under it.

4. What FansX may do

4.1 Where the Customer's use is not fair under clause 3, exceeds the limits of its subscription plan, or falls within clause 2.2, FansX may take any of the following steps:

(a) charge for the excess at FansX's published rates;

(b) apply rate limits or reduce throughput for the affected feature;

(c) require the Customer to move to a plan appropriate to its use;

(d) suspend the affected feature on notice; or

(e) where the use is causing, or is likely to cause, material harm to the Services, to Members or to FansX's standing with a Third-Party Platform, suspend the affected feature immediately, notifying the Customer promptly afterwards.

4.2 FansX will ordinarily contact the Customer before taking a step under clause 4.1(b) to (d), and will work with the Customer to bring its use within the limits where it is willing to do so. Clause 4.1(e) is not subject to that expectation.

4.3 FansX may monitor use of the Services for the purposes of this Policy.

4.4 Nothing in this Policy limits FansX's rights under clauses 8.4, 14.2, 14.3 or 14.4 of the General Terms.

5. Changes

5.1 FansX may update this Policy by publishing an updated version. Where an update materially increases the Customer's obligations or materially restricts its use of the Services, FansX will give at least 30 days' notice before it takes effect for an existing Customer — except that an update required by law, by a regulator, for security reasons or by a Third-Party Platform's requirements may take effect immediately on publication. The Member cap is stated in the Customer's subscription plan and is governed by the General Terms, not by this Policy.
