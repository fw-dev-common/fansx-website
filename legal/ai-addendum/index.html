AI Services Addendum

Version 2.0 · Last updated 12 August 2026

1. Application

1.1 This Addendum applies wherever the Customer uses an AI Feature. Capitalised terms have the meanings given in the FansX Customer Terms of Service and the Data Processing Agreement.

2. Nature of AI Features

2.1 AI Features operate by automated means and are probabilistic. Their output may be incomplete, inaccurate, or may vary between uses or between customers for similar inputs.

2.2 FansX warrants the operation of the AI Features in accordance with clause 11.2 of the General Terms (reasonable skill and care), but does not warrant the outcome of any individual automated decision, on the basis stated in Schedule A4 of the General Terms, which applies to all AI Features. Where an automated decision (for example a receipt validation) is wrong, the correction mechanism in Schedule A applies; the answer to an erroneous outcome is correction, not a claim for the outcome itself.

3. AI service providers

3.1 FansX engages third-party AI and machine-learning service providers to deliver the AI Features. FansX may change those providers from time to time in accordance with clause 8 of the Data Processing Agreement (sub-processors).

4. Customer inputs

4.1 The Customer is responsible for the content it, or its Members, submit to the AI Features.

4.2 The Customer shall not knowingly permit the submission to an AI Feature of material containing full payment-card numbers or identity-card numbers, consistent with clause 7.4 of the Data Processing Agreement.

5. Reliance and oversight

5.1 The Customer shall not rely on the output of an AI Feature as the sole basis for a decision that has a legal or materially adverse effect on a Member. The Customer remains responsible for its programme decisions, including the award, calculation, refusal or withdrawal of points, offers and rewards, whether or not informed by an AI Feature.

5.2 The Customer shall give Members any notice, and obtain any consent, that applicable law requires for the use of AI Features in its programme, and shall use the AI Features in compliance with applicable law, including any law governing automated decision-making about individuals.

6. Model development

6.1 FansX's rights to develop, train, evaluate and improve models are as set out in clauses 6.2 and 7.3 of the Data Processing Agreement, and are confined to the anonymised or aggregated data described in clause 6.1 of that Agreement.

6.2 Where an AI Feature operates through a Third-Party Platform, the Customer acknowledges that the platform's own terms independently restrict the use of data obtained through it, and that the rights referred to in this Addendum are exercised subject to those restrictions.

7. No additional warranty

7.1 Except as expressly stated in clause 2.2, the AI Features are provided on the "as is" basis set out in clause 11.3 of the General Terms, and clause 12 (liability) of the General Terms applies to them.

8. Changes

8.1 FansX may update this Addendum by publishing an updated version, in accordance with the amendment mechanism of the General Terms.
